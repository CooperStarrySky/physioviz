Tissue model: every dot is a fixed amount. 1 blue dot = 0.25 L of water; 1 yellow (Na⁺) or purple (K⁺) dot = 71 mOsm, so normal osmolality is one solute dot per water dot. (Real water molecules outnumber solute particles about 190 to 1.) Dots only enter from upstream blood or leave downstream; each compartment is drawn at a size proportional to its water.
